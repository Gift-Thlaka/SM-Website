Guide of use

Desktop application

  1. Download official application from our website
  2. Install the application on your windows device
  3. You need to have registered store to log in
  4. else Your can create an account for your store
  5. Keep password and email safe
  6. Register your products
  7. register your employees
  8. Manage your shelves

Mobile application

  1. Download official application from our website
  2. Install the application on your android device
  3. You need to be registered employee to log in
  4. else Your employer can register you
  5. Employer will provide your empoyee ID
  6. Keep password safe
  7. Check your device for tasks
  8. Manage shelves
